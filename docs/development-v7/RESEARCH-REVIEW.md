# Research and design review — 12 September 2026

The release distinguishes recorded developmental context from an actual measurement of brain maturation. Questionnaire ages are optional calendar history. Primary studies retain their own age covariates, sampled groups, stimulus conditions and measurement limitations; none supplies a coefficient for individual brain damage, powers, puberty, gender or protective buffering.

## Primary research retained in the new library

- Goddings et al. (2014), [10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.09.073](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3991320/): longitudinal puberty and regional volume; age and puberty can both contribute. No universal maturity cutoff.
- Satterthwaite et al. (2014), [10.1016/j.jaac.2013.12.002](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3935178/): puberty-by-sex interaction for hippocampal morphology, not every region. Cross-sectional; not ability.
- Vijayakumar et al. (2019), [10.1016/j.cortex.2019.04.024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7057923/): emotional-face task, PDS and testosterone. Exploratory condition-specific results and correction limits are retained.
- Gunnar et al. (2019), [10.1073/pnas.1909699116](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31712449/): within-person pubertal progression and cortisol reactivity after institutional deprivation. Sex was a covariate, not a sex-specific treatment rule. Published author correction linked in the card.
- Church et al. (2025), [10.1016/j.dcn.2025.101588](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271767/): assigned sex, stimulus valence and regulation instruction interact. OFC contrasts vary by condition; amygdala follow-up nulls are kept.
- Dhamala et al. (2024), [10.1126/sciadv.adn4202](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn4202): assigned sex and gender questionnaires are distinct. Within-sex models explain about 0.55–0.56% of parent-reported gender variance; self-reported gender prediction was nonsignificant. This is not a stimulus-response study or identity classification.
- Wang et al. (2026, online March), [10.1016/j.biopsych.2026.02.016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41791639/): age/sex-stratified structural findings. Scan age is not exposure stage; pediatric null results are retained. Abstract review is labeled.
- Gee et al. (2013), [10.1073/pnas.1307893110](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1307893110): early environment and circuit-specific functional coupling. Not globally faster or healthier maturation.

All eight cards are available to every reader. Stage-related reading selections are explicitly related context, not matched-exposure or individual-effect findings. Current gender cannot fill in historical gender, assigned sex, hormone values or pubertal stage.

## Visual direction

[Linear’s March 2026 refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh) informed the preference for predictable navigation and restrained secondary detail. No Linear code, visual assets or proprietary layouts were copied. The implemented Cortex Field Guide is an original expression of the approved brain-and-compass mark: navy/sea-glass tones, warm-paper sections, brass orientation details, serif accents, asymmetric control corners, report-edition previews and four-step journey markers.

No external font, analytics or design-library dependency was added. The browser handles image/3D interactions locally. Reduced-motion preferences and visible keyboard focus remain respected; no claim of a full accessibility certification is made.
