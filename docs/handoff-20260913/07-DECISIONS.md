# 07 — Decisions and remaining limits

The user wants actual Studio implementation and durable state, not repeated plans. Keep a current README, exact commits, native deployment receipt, test boundaries and an actionable dependency board. Try available connector tools before declaring them unavailable. An individual denied/slow operation is not evidence that the entire connection disappeared; do not invent its cause or bypass safeguards.

The active biological-sex field is required Male/Female only, starts blank and has no uncertainty/withhold choice. Preserve old missing/identity values without converting them into sex. Other historical questions retain their existing unknown/skip semantics. Avatar presentation is an independent visual choice, not evidence of anatomy or a replacement for biological context.

The experience should be compelling through specificity, beautiful artwork and useful actions. Preserve possible capabilities, demanding conditions, supports, signature moves and testable small experiments. Do not turn every difficulty into a gift or claim that adversity was necessary. No severity/rarity score or more-harm-equals-more-power rule belongs in the implementation.

Accepted artwork is persistent. Generate only on explicit request with bounded cost and idempotency; print, reload, palette changes and report switching reuse existing pixels. A citation update must not silently change a portrait. New visual editions are deliberate and separate from scientific revision.

The user selected xAI instead of OpenAI. The real service is the existing private-pass pilot, not public registration. Anonymous reflection and local encrypted backups are working features. Future account/vault work requires an explicit authentication, recovery, deletion and privacy design before implementation claims.

Gamma authenticated and produced a real two-page fictional comparison for six credits in that run. It altered some wording and needed logo-contrast fixes. The chosen architecture is native rendering for personal/scientific reports, Gamma for fictional/public design work. That test does not authorize automatic transfer of private reports, establish a universal six-credit price or mean Gamma is the live report renderer.

Earlier numerical scope promises were plans, not delivered results. There are not hundreds of validated new interaction rules or thousands of empirically established hero profiles. The preserved graph has 137 nodes, 212 structural links and 351 mathematically enumerable pairs. Its tests are software checks; its weights are hand-set. The live hero engine remains the original 27-observation/24-composition implementation until a reviewed successor is integrated and deployed.

No direct personal neural data, completed systematic meta-analysis, clinical validation, public account system, longitudinal outcomes study or calibrated individual predictor was delivered here. A plausible mechanism or strong self-recognition cannot uniquely identify an individual's anatomy. Keep these limitations precise rather than either overstating certainty or treating all useful hypotheses as impossible.

This handoff changes documentation and preservation/work tracking only. It does not deploy the graph, modify production, renew grants, generate new paid images, or enroll anyone in research. Follow newer user instructions when they change scope and retain the historical decision record.
