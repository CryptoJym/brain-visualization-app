# Cortex Compass — neuroscience-first Neurohero system

Status: active implementation branch `astra/cortex-neurohero-v9` in `/Users/utlyze/Projects/cortex-compass-neurohero-v9`.

Goal: build a deep, evidence-linked hero profile that makes strengths, trade-offs, supports, and possibilities memorable without pretending the questionnaire measured a person's brain.

## Scientific contract

The engine uses six layers:
1. Measurement: what a study or personal task actually measured.
2. Circuit/process: the specific structural, functional, behavioral, or physiological property implicated.
3. Functional process: a defined cognitive/affective process with alternative explanations.
4. Composition: an interaction between processes under specified conditions.
5. Everyday manifestation: reported examples, counterexamples, context, costs, and supports.
6. Hero expression: editorial name, motifs, signature moves, and practical experiments.

History can nominate relevant literature. It may not create a hidden anatomical alteration. Group associations remain group associations. A reported personal pattern remains self-report. A measured personal result must preserve its modality and conditions. No validated individual neurological prediction is claimed by this release.
## Composition rules

Direct joint evidence outranks cross-study hypothesis. Never multiply correlations or add effect sizes from incompatible modalities. A statistically significant result in one subgroup and a nonsignificant result in another is not itself proof of a subgroup difference.

Cross-source hypotheses must expose component evidence, proposed bridge, alternatives, possible trade-off, support conditions, and an observation that would weaken the interpretation. Counterexamples reduce or remove the corresponding hero composition; they are not treated as hidden pathology.

The starting functional dimensions include salience detection, threat/safety discrimination, contextual memory, generalization, sustained attention, attentional disengagement, working-memory updating, inhibition, cognitive flexibility, uncertainty learning, reward learning, interoception, arousal recovery, social-cue discrimination, perspective checking, planning, sequencing, creative recombination, and support/agency processes. The registry is expandable and does not claim these dimensions are independent.

## Hero expression

The hero profile emphasizes useful conditions, signature moves, demanding conditions, support amplifiers, and a small experiment. It never rewards adversity severity or implies someone should be grateful for harm. Shared hero cards default to strengths; private costs, history, demographic context, and uncertainty require explicit sharing.

Visual presentation is chosen by the user independently of sex/gender research fields. Portraits are original adult anime-inspired characters using navy, sea-glass, and brass motifs. The image prompt receives only an allowlisted visual brief, never raw history, diagnoses, contact data, inferred lesions, or the scientific report.

## Portrait persistence

Generate one accepted portrait per visual-edition revision and reuse those exact bytes for later PDFs. Profile evidence revision, art-specification revision, and image-asset revision are separate. Printing never triggers image generation. Regeneration is explicit and quota-bearing.

Anonymous exploration and ordinary reports remain available. Cloud saving and paid portrait generation require authenticated, account-scoped storage, explicit consent, idempotency, quotas, bounded retries, private retrieval, deletion controls, and server-side secrets. If those prerequisites are unavailable, the production feature remains disabled rather than silently weakening privacy or cost controls.
