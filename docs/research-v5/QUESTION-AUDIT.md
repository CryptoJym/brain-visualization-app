# Question-to-study audit

Version: cc-region-evidence-5.0. All 28 existing prompts reviewed.

Domain / related labels describe exposure fit, not clinical certainty. Unmapped means no isolated region assignment in this review, not no research or no importance.

## Physical harm (physical_assault)
**Related evidence**

Family-violence and broader abuse samples are relevant, but do not isolate this one question.

- McCrory et al. (2011) — DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2011.10.015; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22153160/
- Puetz et al. (2020; online 2019) — DOI 10.1017/S003329171900134X; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31190662/
- Wang et al. (2026; online March 5) — DOI 10.1016/j.biopsych.2026.02.016; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41791639/

## Threats of harm (threatened_harm)
**Related evidence**

Threat-only experience was not isolated in this family-violence study.

- McCrory et al. (2011) — DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2011.10.015; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22153160/

## Verbal mistreatment (verbal_harm)
**Domain study**

This study examined parental verbal aggression; the question also permits other adults. The left auditory-association location is more specific than “temporal lobe.”

- Tomoda et al. (2011; online 2010) — DOI 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2010.05.027; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20483374/

## Emotional support (emotional_neglect)
**Domain study**

Emotional neglect has specific reward-response research. Broader neglect findings and mixed exposures are shown separately.

- Hanson, Hariri & Williamson (2015) — DOI 10.1016/j.biopsych.2015.05.010; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26092778/
- Puetz et al. (2020; online 2019) — DOI 10.1017/S003329171900134X; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31190662/
- Wang et al. (2026; online March 5) — DOI 10.1016/j.biopsych.2026.02.016; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41791639/

## Basic material needs (material_needs)
**Related evidence**

Income-based poverty research is not a direct measure of food, clothing or the cause of unmet needs.

- Hair et al. (2015) — DOI 10.1001/jamapediatrics.2015.1475; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26192216/

## Access to needed care (medical_neglect)
**No specific region assigned**

No reviewed study here isolates repeated failure to obtain care from illness, financial access and other circumstances. A specific region is not assigned.


## Safe supervision (supervision_neglect)
**Related evidence**

Institutional deprivation is much broader than this supervision item. Read it as related context, not a one-to-one mapping.

- Bick et al. (2015) — DOI 10.1001/jamapediatrics.2014.3212; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25622303/

## Household alcohol problems (household_alcohol)
**No specific region assigned**

The household-alcohol domain is not the same as prenatal alcohol exposure, the respondent’s own alcohol use or inherited familial risk. These cannot be substituted to assign a region.


## Household drug use (household_drugs)
**No specific region assigned**

Drug type, prenatal exposure, caregiving and the respondent’s own use are different exposures. This item alone does not specify a brain-study match.


## Household incarceration (household_incarceration)
**No specific region assigned**

The reviewed incarceration sources describe health and behavioral outcomes, not an isolated regional brain effect. An important experience can remain unmapped.


## Household mental health (household_mental_health)
**No specific region assigned**

A household member’s mental health, the care environment and familial risk are distinct. A diagnosis in someone else does not assign a brain effect to the respondent.


## Suicide attempt in the household (household_suicide_attempt)
**No specific region assigned**

A household suicide attempt has its own meaning and context. The selected sources do not justify an isolated region assignment from this Yes/No item.


## Caregiver separation (caregiver_separation)
**No specific region assigned**

Separation or divorce is not equivalent to severe institutional deprivation, bereavement or abuse; those imaging findings are not transferred to this item.


## Witnessed caregiver violence (witnessed_violence)
**Domain study**

The visual study required visually witnessed interparental violence. The separate family-violence fMRI study measures threat response, not cortical thickness.

- Tomoda et al. (2012) — DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0052528; https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0052528
- McCrory et al. (2011) — DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2011.10.015; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22153160/

## Peer emotional mistreatment (peer_emotional)
**Domain study**

The source measured broader repeated peer victimization, not a uniquely emotional-bullying neural signature.

- Burke Quinlan et al. (2018 online; 2020 issue) — DOI 10.1038/s41380-018-0297-9; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30542059/

## Peer physical bullying (peer_physical)
**Domain study**

The source measured broader peer victimization. It does not establish a separate physical-bullying effect or an additional effect to add to emotional bullying.

- Burke Quinlan et al. (2018 online; 2020 issue) — DOI 10.1038/s41380-018-0297-9; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30542059/

## Sexual boundaries (sexual_boundary)
**Domain study**

The specific sensory finding came from adult women and a much more detailed abuse measure; other samples may differ.

- Heim et al. (2013) — DOI 10.1176/appi.ajp.2013.12070950; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23732967/
- Puetz et al. (2020; online 2019) — DOI 10.1017/S003329171900134X; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31190662/

## Identity-based mistreatment (discrimination)
**Related evidence**

The study concerns racial discrimination. This broader identity-discrimination question must not be treated as proof of that narrower exposure.

- Chen, Lopez-Quintero & Elton (2026; online 2025) — DOI 10.1016/j.jaac.2025.04.005; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40222403/

## Medical experiences (medical_trauma)
**No specific region assigned**

Serious illness, painful treatment and perceived medical threat differ biologically. No particular illness or neurological injury is assumed from this question.


## Caregiver death (caregiver_death)
**No specific region assigned**

Bereavement, prolonged grief, caregiver disruption and depression are not interchangeable. No reviewed study here supports assigning a regional change to every bereaved child.


## Disaster exposure (disaster)
**No specific region assigned**

Disaster studies often involve particular events and selected symptomatic samples. A generic exposure answer is not enough to infer PTSD-related brain findings.


## Before birth: reported depression (prenatal_depression)
**Related evidence**

Measured prenatal depression and neonatal diffusion findings are shown as separate research context. The reported history does not activate a personal brain prediction.

- Rifkin-Graboi et al. (2013) — DOI 10.1016/j.biopsych.2013.06.019; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23968960/

## Before birth: reported alcohol exposure (prenatal_alcohol)
**Related evidence**

The source studied heavy prenatal exposure. A Yes without dose or verified details cannot inherit that group’s brain-development findings; this is context only.

- Lebel et al. (2012) — DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1161-12.2012; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23115162/

## Dependable adult (safe_adult)
**Related evidence**

Observed maternal support and parental acceptance are related to, but narrower than, any dependable adult. These support studies do not cancel adversity or estimate growth.

- Luby et al. (2012) — DOI 10.1073/pnas.1118003109; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22308421/
- Thorsen et al. (2025, December 18) — DOI 10.1017/S0033291725102651; https://doi.org/10.1017/S0033291725102651

## Trusted friendship (close_friend)
**No specific region assigned**

Friendship is an endorsed support, not a biomarker of a particular brain region. The population support study does not provide an individual neural estimate.


## Belonging (belonging)
**No specific region assigned**

Different sources of belonging should not be collapsed into a single regional neural effect. Keep this as a self-reported support.


## Feeling capable (competence)
**No specific region assigned**

A skill or feeling of competence does not identify a neural superpower or larger brain structure. This remains an explicit personal-strength reflection.


## Grounding routines (routine)
**No specific region assigned**

Predictable routines may feel helpful; this item is not a sleep, stress-hormone or hypothalamus assessment.
